Orange eyebrow + heavy condensed display title for section headers.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Яагаад БЭРД?" accent="давуу тал">Бидний</SectionHeading>
<SectionHeading slant size="display-1">Хувийн дасгалжуулалт</SectionHeading>
```

- `eyebrow` is the wide-tracked orange label. `accent` colours a trailing word orange.
- `slant` adds the poster skew — use sparingly, on punchy headers.
